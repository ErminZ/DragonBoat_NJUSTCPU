疫情之前，药大联合南大便已是波士顿龙舟赛的常驻劲旅。多年连续参赛、屡获佳绩，是校友圈中有口皆碑的高质量社团——不止为奖牌，更为那份"每年夏天，水上相见"的默契。

Before the pandemic, CPU and NJU alumni had already established themselves as a perennial force at the Boston Dragon Boat Festival. Years of consecutive competition and consistent results made the team a beloved institution in the alumni community — not just for the medals, but for that unspoken tradition: meeting on the water every summer.

三年间，队伍从初露锋芒，到稳居强手之列，再到刷新队史纪录，步步为营、节节攀升；越来越多的校友加入其中，让这支队伍不断壮大，也让校友情感在异乡蓬勃生长。

Over three years, the team grew from newcomers to a top-tier competitor, breaking their own records along the way. More and more alumni joined, making the team stronger — and the bonds between alumni abroad even deeper.
